HOW IT WORKS

The AZoom Rental Process

From reservation to final payment — a simple, transparent five-step process.

  1. 01

    Reserve Online

    Browse our fleet and reserve your car with a credit card hold. No charge until pickup.

  2. 02

    Pick Up at Office

    Visit any AZoom rental office to pick up your car. Our staff will get you on the road in minutes.

  3. 03

    Return at Car Park

    Drop off the car at any designated AZoom car park. Just park, lock, and confirm your return.

  4. 04

    Inspection

    Our team inspects the returned vehicle to ensure no damages. You'll be notified of the results.

  5. 05

    Pay Final Bill

    Review your final bill — rental cost plus any damage charges. Pay securely online in seconds.
